# Mayor's Call

You are the mayor of a small town. The chief seismologist says a major earthquake is coming, but not when. Each day, people leave a proposal on your desk. Stamp it **APPROVED** or **DENIED**.

"Call" means the decision, not a phone call.

## How to play

Drag a card left to deny it, or right to approve it. Every stamp moves four meters:

| Meter | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Awareness | Whether people know what to do |
| Money | The town treasury |
| Infrastructure | Buildings, hospital, stores, and crews |
| Bureaucracy | Rules, permits, and inspections |

Each meter starts at 50. Letting one fall to 0 or rise to 100 both end the run. At the end of the day the town shows what your decisions changed. The earthquake arrives on a day you do not know, and the balance at that moment decides the ending. Each run deals a different order of proposals.

## Run the project

Open this repository in Godot 4.7 and press Play. The game starts at the title screen.
